Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker: Minimal & Offline • Version 1.0

Zero Data Collection • 100% Offline

1. Core Privacy Philosophy

Expense Tracker is engineered strictly around the principle of Zero-Knowledge, Zero-Friction Privacy. We believe your personal spending records, ledger entries, and financial history belong exclusively to you.

The app requires no account signup, operates with no cloud servers, and transmits zero network telemetry.

2. Information We Collect

We collect zero personal data.

  • No Cloud Sync: All records entered by you remain stored on your physical device.
  • No Analytics: We do not log screen views, button clicks, or user flow interactions.
  • No Diagnostic Telemetry: We do not harvest device IDs, IP addresses, advertising identifiers, or location data.

3. Local Storage & Encryption

Your transactions and settings are stored locally within the application's isolated OS sandbox:

  • Database Encryption: Stored via SQLite with 256-bit AES encryption (SQLCipher).
  • Sandboxed Isolation: Protected by iOS and Android system sandboxing so third-party applications cannot read your database.

4. Biometric Security

If you enable Face ID, Touch ID, or Android Biometric Authentication:

  • Authentication is handled strictly on-device by your hardware Secure Enclave / Android Keystore.
  • Expense Tracker never accesses, stores, or transmits your biometric samples or raw scan data.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Expense Tracker is 100% free of third-party trackers. The application contains:

  • NO Google Analytics or Firebase Telemetry
  • NO Meta/Facebook SDKs
  • NO Advertising networks or promotional affiliate code

6. Data Ownership & Export

You have full ownership of your data at all times. You can export your complete ledger as a standard .csv spreadsheet with one tap. Deleting the application immediately and irreversibly wipes all local records from your device.
